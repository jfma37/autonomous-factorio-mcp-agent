# Factorio 2.0 Vanilla + Stacking Pro Ratios (45 SPM Target)

## Belt Saturation (Items/s)
- Yellow Belt: 15/s unstacked | 60/s stacked (4-high via stack inserters) | 30 electric drills to saturate
- Red Belt: 30/s unstacked | 120/s stacked (4-high) | 60 electric drills to saturate
- Blue Belt: 45/s unstacked | 180/s stacked (4-high) | 90 electric drills to saturate

## Smelting Columns (Full Belt Saturation)
- 48 Stone Furnaces (24/side) = 15 plates/s (saturates 1 yellow belt; needs 30 electric drills)
- 48 Steel/Electric Furnaces (24/side) = 30 plates/s (saturates 1 red belt; needs 60 electric drills)
- 48 Stone/Steel Furnaces on Iron Plates = 3 steel/s (1/5 of a yellow belt)

## Power Ratios
- Steam Power: 1 Offshore Pump -> 20 Boilers -> 40 Steam Engines = 36 MW (consumes ~11.1 coal/s)
- Solar/Accumulator: 25 Solar Panels : 21 Accumulators = 1.05 MW continuous
- Nuclear (2x2): 4 Reactors -> 48 Heat Exchangers -> 83 Steam Turbines = 480 MW

## Core Circuits & Oil (Assembler 2s)
- Green Circuits: 3 Copper Cable : 2 Electronic Circuit (outputs 3.0/s; needs 3 copper/s + 2 iron/s)
- Red Circuits: 1 Copper Cable : 6 Advanced Circuit (outputs 0.75/s; 1 Plastic chem plant feeds 12 Red Circuit assemblers)
- Advanced Oil: 8 Refineries : 2 Heavy->Light Cracking : 7 Light->Petroleum Cracking : 1 Water Pump
- Batteries: 1 Sulfur Plant -> 1 Sulfuric Acid Plant -> 11 Battery Plants

## 45 SPM Science Block Ratios (Assembler 2s, speed 0.75)
- Red Science (Automation): 5 Assemblers (1 Gear assembler feeds up to 10 Red Science)
- Green Science (Logistic): 6 Assemblers (1 Inserter + 1 Belt assembler, fed by 1 Gear + 1 Green Circuit)
- Black Science (Military): SKIP (peaceful mode, no enemies)
- Blue Science (Chemical): 12 Assemblers (10 Engine Unit + 6 Red Circuit + 1 Sulfur plant)
- Purple Science (Production): 7 Assemblers (1 Electric Furnace + 1 Prod Module 1 + 0.5 Rail)
- Yellow Science (Utility): 7 Assemblers (3 Flying Robot Frame + 3 Low Density Structure + 2 Processing Unit)
- Labs: 12-15 Labs chain-fed via inserters (insert 4x Productivity Module 1s when available)

## Rocket Silo Endgame
- 1 Rocket = 100 Rocket Parts = 1,000 LDS + 1,000 Rocket Fuel + 1,000 Processing Units + 1 Satellite.
- ALWAYS insert 4x Productivity Module 3s into the Rocket Silo (~28% material reduction).
